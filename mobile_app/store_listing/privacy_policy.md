# Chess Platform Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Chess Platform stores account details you provide (email, display name and optional profile information), games, ratings, social interactions, moderation reports, and device notification tokens. This information is used to provide authentication, multiplayer chess, history, analysis, fair-play protection, and notifications.

The Android app stores login credentials in Android's encrypted credential storage. Game and account data is sent only to the Chess Platform server URL configured in the app. Firebase Cloud Messaging receives a device token and notification payloads when push notifications are enabled. We do not sell personal information.

You can disable notifications in Android settings. Contact the operator of your configured Chess Platform server to request access, correction, export, or deletion of account data. Server operators must replace this paragraph with their contact email and retention policy before publishing.

The service is not directed to children under the minimum digital-consent age applicable in their country. Security measures are used to protect data, but no network service is risk-free. Material policy changes will be posted at the public privacy-policy URL and reflected by the date above.
